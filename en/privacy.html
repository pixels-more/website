Our apps

What data do the apps collect?

None. Our apps do not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There are no analytics tools, no tracking, no ads and no sign-in.

Local storage

Game saves, settings, high scores and your own creations (such as songs or pictures) are stored on your device only. They never leave it unless you export or share them yourself.

Internet connection

The apps work without an internet connection. A link such as “Buy me a Coffee” merely opens your browser.

App stores

When you download an app from an app store such as Google Play, or buy something there, the store operator processes data under its own privacy policy. We have no influence on this.

Children

Since the apps collect no data, no data is collected from children either.

This website

Hosting

This website is served via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and reliable delivery (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

No cookies, no third-party content

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other servers (not even fonts).

External links

Links to other sites, such as Buy me a Coffee or an app store, lead to services of other providers. Their privacy policies apply there.

Contact by email

If you write to us, we use your details only to answer your request and delete them once they are no longer needed for that.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
